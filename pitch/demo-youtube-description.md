DEMO PATH 1 — S1 Discovery Market (Mika Zhou)

The S1 market is where fans back early creators using non-transferable SPUMP tokens. This demo shows:

- Bonding curve pricing — current price, next price, and price history chart
- Market stats — S1 supply, holder count, graduation progress, supporter pool
- Buy and sell flows — amount selection, estimated cost/return, and position updates
- Position tracking — S1 balance, cost basis, and claimable USDC status

SPUMP is burned into internal creator positions on the bonding curve. No tradable creator token is minted. The oracle adjusts creator ratings based on followers, views, and growth velocity.

---

DEMO PATH 2 — S1 Buyout (Luna Cai)

When a creator reaches critical mass, sponsors compete with USDC buyout offers. This demo shows:

- Phase stepper — Offers Open, Accepted, Rage Quit Window, Graduated
- Buyout metrics — accepted offer ($850K), winning sponsor, USDC deposited, claimable amounts
- Rage quit countdown — 48-hour window where backers can exit at full curve price, zero exit tax
- Sponsor offers table — competing bids with status (Accepted vs Outbid)
- Rage quit action — exit S1 positions and receive SPUMP back with no penalty
- USDC claim — pro-rata payout from the buyout escrow vault
- Portfolio verification — positions, cost basis, and claim queue

The buyout converts fan conviction into real USDC. Early backers receive a capped bonus share.

Graduated creators enter S2 — the structured sponsorship market. This demo covers:
