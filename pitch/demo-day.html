The philosophy · lifecycles & seasons

A creator's needs change as they grow. So the product has two seasons. 创作者的需求随成长而变 —— 所以产品分两季。

Season 1 · Discovery

Early — no track record yet

Brands won't touch them. What they need is the crowd: fans and scouts who discover them, back them with skin in the game, and prove the content has value.

早期没有履历,品牌不理。他们需要的是人群 —— 粉丝和星探用真金白银发现并背书,证明内容有价值。

Season 2 · Sponsored campaigns

Momentum — time to scale

Now the challenge is scale. They need sponsors with real USDC ad budgets to fund campaigns and grow the IP.

有了势能,挑战变成规模化。他们需要带真实 USDC 预算的赞助商,投放活动、把 IP 做大。

Content 内容 → Momentum 热度 → Fan backing 粉丝早投 → Sponsor USDC 赞助预算 → Solana settle 链上结算

StreamPump 把这两个阶段连成一个 Web2.5 闭环 —— 社交级体验,链上结算真值,跑在 Solana 上。
