StreamPump — The Web2.5 Creator Sponsorship Market on Solana | Colosseum Hackathon 2026

StreamPump is a Web2.5 creator sponsorship market where content discovery, sponsor budgets, fan participation, and on-chain settlement live in one product loop. Built on Solana.

This video walks through the StreamPump hackathon pitch — what we're building, why the creator economy needs it, and how Solana powers every core mechanism.

---

THE PROBLEM

The creator economy is projected to reach $480B by 2027 (Goldman Sachs), yet 99% of creators can't make a living. MCN agencies take 15–30% of sponsorship revenue. There are no market signals before content production. And settlement is still stuck on flat fees and click KPIs.

Meanwhile, Web3 social platforms have collapsed — Friend.tech lost 87% of its users, Farcaster signups dropped 96%, and Lens new users fell 99.6%. Token-first incentives, view-to-earn sell pressure, and impractical on-chain content storage are structural failures.

---

THE SOLUTION

StreamPump connects three roles — creators, fans, and sponsors — through two product seasons:

Season 1: Creator Discovery Market
Fans burn non-transferable SPUMP (Token-2022) into creator positions priced by a rating-adjusted bonding curve. An oracle evaluates creator momentum (followers, views, growth). When a creator reaches critical mass, sponsors compete to buy them out — and fans earn pro-rata USDC.

Season 2: Sponsored Campaign Market
Three budget tracks give sponsors granular control:
• Track 1 — Fixed base pay (guaranteed creator income)
• Track 2 — Performance-driven (views/clicks/saves + cliff + fan endorsement pool)
• Track 3 — CPS with delayed settlement window

All campaign funds are held in Solana PDA-owned USDC vaults. Settlement is on-chain. Content stays fast off-chain.

---

WHY SOLANA

Solana is not decorative — it's a product requirement:
• Sub-second finality for real-time bonding curve UX
• Under $0.01 per tx makes micro-settlement economically viable
• Token-2022 NonTransferable extension enforces SPUMP as utility-only
• PDA architecture for composable on-chain state (positions, proposals, vaults)
• Anchor framework: 20+ type-safe instructions covering the full product lifecycle
