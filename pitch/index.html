Season 2

Sponsored Campaign Market

Three budget tracks give sponsors granular control while guaranteeing creator income and fan participation.

TRACK 1

Fixed Base Pay

Guaranteed creator compensation. Sponsor commits USDC upfront. Oracle settles to creator's payout ATA. One-time, unconditional.

TRACK 2

Performance + Fan Endorsement

Budget tied to views, clicks, saves. Includes a cliff threshold — below it, full refund to sponsor. Above it, 80% to creator + 20% fan endorsement pool. Fans burn SPUMP to endorse and earn pro-rata USDC rewards.

TRACK 3

CPS with Delayed Settlement

Cost-per-sale model. Sponsor pre-funds estimated budget. After the refund window closes, approved payout goes to creator. Unused balance returns to sponsor. Overflow generates an invoice for the difference.
